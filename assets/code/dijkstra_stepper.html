How Dijkstra works: Start at S and explore outward. Blue cells = discovered but not yet visited — their number is the cheapest known cost to reach them from S. Each step we visit the blue cell with the smallest cost (guaranteed shortest). Then we look at its neighbors: if going through this cell gives a cheaper path to a neighbor, we update that neighbor's cost. Gray cells = already visited (finalized).
